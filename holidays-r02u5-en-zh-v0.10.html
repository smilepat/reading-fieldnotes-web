      New words, with the book's meanings: 新词（书中的释义）

        In this unit, Tao’s aunt and uncle are his father’s sister and her husband, and Tao’s mother goes with the children on the train there and back. Words A teaches holiday as one day with no school; “the summer holiday” in Read A is many weeks. “Welcome it” in Read B means to be happy when the new year comes. Read B is about ringing a big temple bell 108 times on the last night of the year, a folk custom at old temples in China, Japan and other places in East Asia. At Hanshan Temple in Suzhou, the bell is struck 108 times on December 31, and the last stroke comes at midnight. One reason given in China: 108 = 12 months + 24 solar terms + 72 five-day periods. In China there are two new years: 元旦 and 春节. 本单元中 Tao 的姑妈是他爸爸的姐妹，姑父是她的丈夫；Tao 的妈妈陪孩子们坐火车往返。Words A 中 holiday 指一天不上学，Read A 的 the summer holiday 指好几个星期的暑假；Read B 的 welcome it 指高高兴兴地迎接新年。Read B 讲的是除夕夜在古寺敲钟一百零八下的民俗，中国、日本等东亚地方都有。苏州寒山寺每年 12 月 31 日夜敲钟 108 下，最后一下正好在零点。中国常见的一种说法是：108 = 12 个月 + 24 节气 + 72 候。中国有两个新年：元旦和春节，可以问问学生两个晚上各做什么。
