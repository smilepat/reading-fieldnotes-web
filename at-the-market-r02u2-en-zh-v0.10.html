      New words, with the book's meanings: 新词（书中的释义）

        The story uses “coins” for money; use the money of your country when you talk with the class. Read B is about the mujin hanbaijo (“no-person sales places”) of Japan: farmers leave fresh vegetables at a small stand by the road or the field, and buyers leave money in a box. Prices are often about 100 yen a bag (about 5 yuan). Most buyers pay, but not all (one Japanese government site says 80–90% of the money comes in). China has stands like this, too: 诚信摊 / 无人售货摊. Skill B on this page: the time word or order word is chosen from four; the book also accepts “in the morning” for the third line. 故事里用“硬币”表示钱，和学生讨论时可以换成人民币。Read B 讲的是日本的“无人贩卖所”：农民把新鲜蔬菜放在路边或田边的小货摊上，买的人把钱放进箱子里，一袋常常约 100 日元（约 5 元人民币）。大多数人会付钱，但不是所有人。中国很多地方也有“诚信摊”“无人售货摊”，可以问问学生有没有见过。本页 Skill B 从四个词语中选择；书中第三行也接受 in the morning。
