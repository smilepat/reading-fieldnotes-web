      Think and talk · A 想一想，说一说

      1. Food falls and falls. Is the little girl the problem, or is the machine the problem? Give a reason from the story. 狗粮掉个不停。问题在小女孩，还是在机器？用故事里的话说明理由。

      I think ______ is the problem, because the story says ______.

      2. Do you often forget things? What do you do about it? 你常常忘事吗？你会怎么办？

      I often forget ______. I ______.

      Say it to your partner first. Then write it if you want. 先说给同桌听，想写再写下来。

      Think and talk · B 想一想，说一说

      1. The writer says the internet was “not very useful” in 1969. Is it useful now? Give a reason from the text. 作者说 1969 年的互联网“不太有用”。现在它有用吗？用课文里的话说明理由。

      I think ______, because the text says ______.

      2. Can you live for one week without a phone or the internet? Why, or why not? 你能一个星期不用电话和互联网吗？为什么？

      I can / I can’t, because ______.

      Say it to your partner first. Then write it if you want. 先说给同桌听，想写再写下来。

      New words, with the book's meanings: 新词（书中的释义）

        Skill A is skimming Read B, so the book does it in Lesson 2, after Words B and before Read B; on this page it sits in Check A (8–10, Your turn), and its “Read paragraph” buttons open Read B. Read B is about the first message on the ARPANET, 29 October 1969: Charley Kline at UCLA sent “LOGIN” to the Stanford Research Institute; “L” and “O” arrived, then the computer crashed. About an hour later the full word got through. At the same institute, Douglas Engelbart’s team invented the computer mouse, which first had two wheels. In December 1969 only four computers were on the network. The order question in Check A (11) is not in the book; it was made for this page. Skill A 是快速浏览 Read B，书中安排在第 2 课时（学完 Words B、读 Read B 之前）；本页把它放在 Check A（第 8–10 题和 Your turn），点“Read paragraph”会打开 Read B。Read B 讲 1969 年 10 月 29 日阿帕网上的第一条消息：加州大学洛杉矶分校的学生向斯坦福研究所发送 LOGIN，只收到 L 和 O，电脑就死机了，约一小时后才发送成功。同一研究所恩格尔巴特的团队发明了鼠标，最早的鼠标底下有两个轮子。1969 年 12 月网上只有四台电脑。Check A 第 11 题（排序）是本页新增的，书中没有。
