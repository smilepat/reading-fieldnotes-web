      New words, with the book's meanings: 新词（书中的释义）

        Guess the word (Skill A): fever, stomach, medicine and throat are not unit words; students guess them from the next sentence. Read B is about a sheep festival in a big city in late fall: farmers (shepherds) walk their sheep through the city center, the city closes the streets, and the police keep a way open. The text does not name the city. fall = autumn: this series uses American English. Skill A 的 fever、stomach、medicine、throat 不是本单元新词，让学生根据下一句猜意思。Read B 讲的是秋天大城市里的赶羊节：农民（牧羊人）赶着羊穿过市中心，城市封路，警察保持道路畅通。文中没有说城市的名字。本系列用美式英语，fall = autumn（秋天）。

          English first on the student pages 学生页以英语为主

          • The texts, the questions, the choices and the activity words are in English only. 课文、题目、选项和活动用语只用英文。
          • Chinese help is off when the page opens. The “中文帮助” switch shows Chinese notes about how to use the page, not the answers. 打开页面时中文帮助是关闭的。打开后显示的是操作说明，不是答案。
          • Students can ask for Chinese one piece at a time: a word's meaning (second tap), a hint for one question, and the paragraph translations of Read A or Read B after all the questions of that part are answered and checked. 学生可以逐项求助：单词的中文释义（第二次点击）、单道题的提示；A 或 B 的段落译文要等该部分的题全部答完并核对后才开放。
