Warm-up: When, Where, and What

A day has many parts. For each part, ask: Where are they now? What do they do there? 一天分成好几个部分。每个部分都问一问：他们现在在哪里？在那里做什么？

Watch: I read, “It is summer.” When? In summer. Where do they go? To the sea.

Read the story again. Write one word on each line. 再读一遍故事，每条横线上写一个词。

      New words, with the book's meanings: 新词（书中的释义）

        Background for Read B (Teacher Notes): Read B is about a train ferry. The best-known one in China crosses the Qiongzhou Strait between Guangdong and Hainan: the train is cut into a few parts, small engines push the parts onto rails inside the ship, and passengers stay on the train. The crossing takes about one hour. The Grade 3 text does not name the place and does not say that the train is cut into parts. Buses and cars cross on other ships; the text only says a bus waits there. Read B 讲的是火车轮渡。中国最有名的是跨越琼州海峡、连接广东和海南的火车轮渡：火车被分成几节，由小机车推上船内的铁轨，乘客留在车上，航程大约一小时。课文没有说出地名，也没有说火车被分成几节。
