      I think ______, because the text says ______.

      2. Which animal in your country needs help? What can people do for it? 我们国家哪种动物需要帮助？人们能为它做什么？

      New words, with the book's meanings: 新词（书中的释义）

        Read B is about Pere David’s deer (milu, also called 四不像). The text says “kings” for the emperors; the park is the old imperial hunting park at Nanyuan, south of Beijing. In 1894 a flood of the Yongding River broke the park wall, and in 1900 soldiers of the Eight-Nation Alliance killed the last deer. The 11th Duke of Bedford brought 18 deer together at Woburn Abbey in England; in 1985 about 20 came back to the Nanhaizi Milu Park, and in 1986 more went to Dafeng by the sea in Jiangsu. Today there are more than 10,000 in China, about 2,900–4,400 of them wild. In Read A, Kai’s camera light is off for the animals. The order question in Check A (11) is not in the book; it was made for this page. Read B 讲的是麋鹿（四不像）。课文里的 kings 指清朝皇帝；公园是北京南边的南苑皇家猎苑。1894 年永定河洪水冲垮了围墙，1900 年八国联军打死了最后的麋鹿。英国贝德福德公爵把 18 只麋鹿集中养在乌邦寺；1985 年约 20 只回到南海子麋鹿苑，1986 年又有一批去了江苏大丰的海边保护区。现在中国有一万多只麋鹿，野生的约 2900～4400 只。Check A 第 11 题（排序）是本页新增的，书中没有。
