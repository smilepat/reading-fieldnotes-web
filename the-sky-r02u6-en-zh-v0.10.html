      Think and talk · A 想一想，说一说

      Mei wants to visit space one day. Do you want to go into space? Why or why not? Mei 想有一天去太空。你想去太空吗？为什么？

      I ______ to go into space, because ______.

      Say it to your partner first. Then write it if you want. 先说给同桌听，想写再写下来。

      Think and talk · B 想一想，说一说

      Think about the old starlight in the text. How does it make you feel? Why? 想一想课文里很“老”的星光。它让你有什么感觉？为什么？

      It makes me feel ______, because ______.

      Say it to your partner first. Then write it if you want. 先说给同桌听，想写再写下来。

      New words, with the book's meanings: 新词（书中的释义）

        Unit 5 taught holiday as one day with no school; here “the summer holiday” is many weeks with no school. Read B: sunlight takes about 8 minutes 20 seconds to reach the earth; a car at 100 km an hour would need about 171 years; light goes about 7.5 times around the earth in a second; moonlight is sunlight the moon sends back (about 1.3 seconds); the nearest star after the sun is about 4.2 light years away. Never look straight at the sun, even with sunglasses. China’s Chang’e robots have landed on the moon, and China plans to send people there before 2030. The order question in Check A (10) is not in the book; it was made for this page. 第 5 单元学过 holiday 指一天不上学；这里 the summer holiday 指好几个星期的暑假。Read B：阳光约 8 分 20 秒到达地球；时速 100 公里的汽车要开约 171 年；光 1 秒约绕地球 7.5 圈；月光是月亮反射的太阳光（约 1.3 秒）；除太阳外最近的恒星约 4.2 光年远。即使戴墨镜也不要直视太阳。中国的嫦娥探测器已经登上月球，中国计划在 2030 年前实现载人登月。Check A 第 10 题（排序）是本页新增的，书中没有。
