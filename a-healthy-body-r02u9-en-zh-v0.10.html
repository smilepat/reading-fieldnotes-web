      Think and talk · A 想一想，说一说

      1. Is it ever okay to go to bed late? When? Think about what happens to Tao in the story. 晚睡有没有可以的时候？什么时候？想一想故事里 Tao 怎么样了。

      It is okay when ______, but the story shows that ______.

      2. What do you do when you are tired after school? 放学后累了，你会做什么？

      When I am tired, I ______, because ______.

      Say it to your partner first. Then write it if you want. 先说给同桌听，想写再写下来。

      Think and talk · B 想一想，说一说

      1. For a long time, there was no good medicine for tooth pain. What can people do for tooth pain today? Give a reason from the text or from your life. 很长时间里都没有治牙疼的好药。今天人们牙疼时可以怎么做？用课文或生活中的例子说明理由。

      People can ______, because ______.

      2. Which do you want to try: salt water, a pig-hair brush, or a brush with a computer? Why? 盐水、猪毛牙刷、带电脑的牙刷，你想试哪一个？为什么？

      I want to try ______, because ______.

      Say it to your partner first. Then write it if you want. 先说给同桌听，想写再写下来。

      New words, with the book's meanings: 新词（书中的释义）

        In Read A, Mei’s mother is a doctor (Book 1, Unit 10). Read B: in China, people cleaned their teeth with salt long before brushes. Brushes of stiff pig hair in a bone or bamboo handle were made in China; sources give different dates (bone handles from the Liao dynasty, about 959; a brush from 1498 is often named as the first), so the text says “over 500 years ago” and “one of the first brushes”. Bacteria in the mouth turn sugar into acid, and the acid makes holes. China’s space station Tiangong goes around the earth about 15–16 times a day. The order question in Check A (12) is not in the book; it was made for this page. Read A 中 Mei 的妈妈是医生（第一册第 10 单元）。Read B：在有牙刷以前，中国人用盐清洁牙齿。用骨柄或竹柄加硬猪鬃做的牙刷最早出现在中国，年代说法不一（辽代约 959 年的骨柄、1498 年明代的牙刷常被称为最早的牙刷），所以课文说“五百多年前”“最早的牙刷之一”。口腔里的细菌把糖变成酸，酸会蛀出洞。中国空间站“天宫”一天绕地球约 15～16 圈。Check A 第 12 题（排序）是本页新增的，书中没有。
