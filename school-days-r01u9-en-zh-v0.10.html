      New words, with the book's meanings: 新词（书中的释义）

        Tao is new in this unit: he comes from a small town by the sea, and his father has a boat (the Grade 3 text says only the boat). In Words A, class is a group of students with one teacher; in Read B, “class time” and “after class” mean a lesson. Read B: boat schools started in 2002 (Shidhulai Swanirvar Sangstha, Bangladesh). In the rainy season, water closes the roads, so boats pick up children along the river and hold class on board; solar panels on the roof give power for lights and computers. The text does not name the country. Tao 是本单元新出现的人物：他来自海边的小镇，爸爸有一条船（课文只提到船）。Words A 里的 class 指“班级”；Read B 里的 class time、after class 指“上课”。Read B 讲的是船上学校：雨季时道路被水淹没，船沿河接孩子并在船上上课，船顶的太阳能板为电灯和电脑供电。课文没有说是哪个国家。
