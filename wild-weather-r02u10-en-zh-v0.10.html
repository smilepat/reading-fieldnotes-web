      New words, with the book's meanings: 新词（书中的释义）

        Skill A is skimming Read B, so the book does it in Lesson 2, after Words B and before Read B; on this page it sits in Check A (8–11, Your turn), and its “Read paragraph” buttons open Read B. Temperatures are in degrees Celsius. Read B is about the Bactrian (two-humped) camel of the Gobi Desert: summer days can go above 40 degrees and winter nights to about 30 degrees below zero. A camel lets its body get several degrees warmer in the day, so it saves water. Wild Bactrian camels (only about 1,000 are left) can drink very salty water. The humps hold fat, not water. In winter the camels eat snow, and in spring the thick coat comes off in big pieces. The Gobi is mostly stony ground, so the text says “hot and dry”. The order question in Check A (12) is not in the book; it was made for this page. Skill A 是快速浏览 Read B，书中安排在第 2 课时（学完 Words B、读 Read B 之前）；本页把它放在 Check A（第 8–11 题和 Your turn），点“Read paragraph”会打开 Read B。温度单位是摄氏度。Read B 讲戈壁沙漠的双峰骆驼：夏天白天能超过 40 度，冬天夜里能到零下 30 度左右。骆驼白天让体温升高几度，可以少出汗、省水。野骆驼（只剩约 1000 峰）能喝很咸的水。驼峰里是脂肪，不是水。冬天骆驼吃雪，春天厚毛一大块一大块地脱落。戈壁大多是石头地，所以课文说“又热又干”。Check A 第 12 题（排序）是本页新增的，书中没有。
