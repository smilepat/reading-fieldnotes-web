Warm-up: Who, Where, and What

When you read a story, ask three questions. Who is in the story? Where are they? What do they do? 读故事时问三个问题：故事里有谁？他们在哪里？他们做什么？

Watch: I read, “He eats it. He is very happy.” Who? Kai. What does he do? He eats his tomato.

Read the story again. Write one word on each line. 再读一遍故事，每条横线上写一个词。

      New words, with the book's meanings: 新词（书中的释义）

        warm has no tap help in Read B, because Check B question 4 asks its meaning. Some students may say “A tomato is a fruit.” Both answers are fine; people cook and eat tomatoes like vegetables. Read B is about young sunflowers: they turn with the sun in the day (a 2016 study in Science, Atamian and others). “Looks at the sun” is a simple way to say this. The word “sunflower” is not taught here; students only need “flower”. Check B 第 4 题考 warm 的词义，所以 Read B 里 warm 不能点击查看释义。有的学生会说“西红柿是水果”，两种说法都可以，我们平时一般把西红柿当蔬菜来做菜。Read B 讲的是小向日葵：白天它们会跟着太阳转（2016 年《Science》的研究）。“look at the sun”是简单的说法；这里不教 sunflower，学生只需要认识 flower。
