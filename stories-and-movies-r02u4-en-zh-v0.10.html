      New words, with the book's meanings: 新词（书中的释义）

        Read B is about stop-motion movies made with clay figures. Movies show 24 pictures a second; clay movies are often shot “on twos”, so one second needs about 12 different poses. At Aardman Animations (Wallace and Gromit), one animator makes about two to four seconds of movie on a good day. Sound and music are added after the pictures are shot. Skill A on this page: the place or time is chosen from four phrases; the book also accepts “to the library” for the third line (in the morning). Read B 讲的是用黏土人偶拍的定格动画。电影每秒放 24 张画面；黏土动画常常每个动作拍两格，所以一秒约要 12 个不同的动作。在制作《超级无敌掌门狗》的 Aardman 工作室，一位动画师顺利的一天约能做 2～4 秒。声音和音乐是拍完画面后才加上的。本页的 Skill A 从四个短语中选择地点或时间；书中第三行（in the morning）也接受 to the library。
