      New words, with the book's meanings: 新词（书中的释义）

        Background for Read B (Teacher Notes): a cat's eyes shine in a light at night because of a mirror-like layer at the back of the eye. Each ear can turn on its own. A cat's claws curve back, so climbing down is hard; many cats come down back feet first, or they jump. A cat's ears often go flat when it is afraid.

        Pronouns to point out: in English Coco is he (a pet with a name), but the Chinese translation uses 它, as Chinese usually does for animals. Read B paragraph 1 has two kinds of it: “It is night” (the time) and “It is a cat” (the thing we see) — a good link to the last line of the It and They rule. 英语里 Coco 用 he（有名字的宠物），中文译文按习惯用“它”。Read B 第 1 段有两种 it：“It is night”指时间，“It is a cat”指看到的东西，可以和 It/They 规则的最后一句联系起来讲。
