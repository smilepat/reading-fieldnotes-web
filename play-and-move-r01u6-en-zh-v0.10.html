Warm-up: Who, Where, When, and What

Ask four questions about a story: Who? Where? When? What happens? The answer to “What happens?” is the big thing in the story. 关于故事问四个问题：谁？在哪里？什么时候？发生了什么？“发生了什么”的答案就是故事里最重要的事。

Watch: I read, “Then Kai has the ball.” Who? Kai. What happens? He has the ball. This is a small thing. The big thing comes next.

Read the story again. Write one word on each line. 再读一遍故事，每条横线上写一个词。
