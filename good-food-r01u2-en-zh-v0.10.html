Warm-up: Who, Where, and What

A story can have two or three places. Ask: Who is there? Where are they now? What do they do there? 一个故事可以有两三个地点。问一问：谁在那里？他们现在在哪里？他们在那里做什么？

Watch: I read, “They eat the soup.” Who? Mei and her family. Where? In the kitchen. What do they do? They eat the soup.

Read the story again. Write one word on each line. 再读一遍故事，每条横线上写一个词。
