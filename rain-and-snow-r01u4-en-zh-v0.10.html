Warm-up: Who, Where, When, and What

      New words, with the book's meanings: 新词（书中的释义）

        fall = autumn. Chinese textbooks often say “autumn”; this series uses American English, so tell students that fall = autumn. In Words A and B, “falls” is a verb (rain falls, snow falls); point this out. 中国教材常用 autumn，本套书用美式英语 fall，请告诉学生 fall = autumn（秋天）。Words A 和 B 句子里的 falls 是动词（雨、雪落下），请提醒学生注意。
