      New words, with the book's meanings: 新词（书中的释义）

        Read B is about Oxford ragwort. It grows wild on the black volcanic rocks of Mount Etna in Sicily, Italy, and was grown in the Oxford Botanic Garden from about 1700. It reached Oxford railway station after the railway came in 1844. The stones between the rails were like its volcanic home, and the wind from passing trains carried its light seeds along the lines. A field is open land with grass or plants. Read B 讲的是“牛津千里光”：它原本长在意大利西西里岛埃特纳火山的黑色岩石上，约 1700 年被种进牛津植物园，1844 年铁路通到牛津后长到了火车站。铁轨间的碎石像它家乡的火山石，火车带起的风把它很轻的种子沿着铁路吹到各地。
