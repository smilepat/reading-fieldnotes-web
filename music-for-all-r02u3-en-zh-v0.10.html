      New words, with the book's meanings: 新词（书中的释义）

        Read B is about “Play Me, I’m Yours”, a street piano project by the British artist Luke Jerram (the text says “a man”). It began in Birmingham, England, in March 2008 with 15 pianos for about three weeks; about 140,000 people played or listened. Over the years, more than 2,000 street pianos went to more than 70 cities. The order question in Check A (10) is not in the book; it was made for this page. Read B 讲的是英国艺术家 Luke Jerram 的街头钢琴项目“Play Me, I’m Yours”（课文只说“一个人”）：2008 年 3 月在英格兰伯明翰开始，15 架钢琴放了约三个星期，约 14 万人弹琴或听琴；这些年来，两千多架街头钢琴去了 70 多个城市。Check A 第 10 题（排序）是本页新增的，书中没有。
