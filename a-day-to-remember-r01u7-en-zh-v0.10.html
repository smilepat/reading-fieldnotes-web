          English first on the student pages 学生页以英语为主

          • The texts, the questions, the choices and the activity words are in English only. 课文、题目、选项和活动用语只用英文。
          • Chinese help is off when the page opens. The “中文帮助” switch shows Chinese notes about how to use the page, not the answers. 打开页面时中文帮助是关闭的。打开后显示的是操作说明，不是答案。
          • Students can ask for Chinese one piece at a time: a word's meaning (second tap), a hint for one question, and the paragraph translations of Read A or Read B after all the questions of that part are answered and checked. 学生可以逐项求助：单词的中文释义（第二次点击）、单道题的提示；A 或 B 的段落译文要等该部分的题全部答完并核对后才开放。
